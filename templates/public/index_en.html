About Us

Allez Climbing Gym started in 2021 on the fourth floor of the Anime Building in Luohu. Back then it was just a group of climbers looking for a place to climb, chat, and hang out. More and more people came, and it grew into what it is today.

We have 450 sqm of bouldering, top-rope, and lead walls, with over 80 routes that get reset regularly. Easy ones, hard ones — beginners have fun, and experienced climbers find their challenges. All our coaches are certified, and we keep an AED on site. Safety is never a compromise here.

But Allez is more than a climbing gym. Our co-founder Edison is always on the move — leading beach clean-ups, organizing swap meets and English salons, and, of course, taking climbers outdoors to real rock. If you'd like to try outdoor climbing, just ask him. Many people say what makes Allez special is the people. Whether you climb well or not, whether you have a partner or not, someone will say hi when you walk in. Climbing shouldn't be a solo thing anyway.

Come by, climb a bit, chat a bit, and meet a few new friends.
