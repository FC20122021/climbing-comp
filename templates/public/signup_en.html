📋 Competition Rules

1. Format

  1. This is a bouldering competition with several routes; the exact number will be announced on site.
  2. Climbers must finish within the designated time; otherwise the score is invalid.
  3. Each route has a Zone and a Top:
    • Top: 25 points
    • Zone only: 10 points
    • No scoring point reached: 0 points
  4. Each failed attempt deducts 0.1 point. Deductions only apply if the climber ultimately reaches a Top or Zone. If no scoring point is reached, no deduction is made.
  5. Ranking is by total score. Ties are broken by Top count, then Zone count, then fewer failed attempts. If still tied, ranks are shared.

2. Fees & Entry

  1. There is no competition fee.
  2. Participants must be members of Allez Climbing Gym and check in normally to join.
  3. Members using a punch card will have one punch deducted.
  4. Monthly, quarterly, and annual members enter normally with no extra charge.
  5. Non-members must purchase a membership before joining the competition.

3. Flow

  1. Arrive 15 minutes early. Late arrivals are considered no-shows.
  2. Follow the judge's and staff's instructions and queue in order.
  3. After finishing a route, return to the back of the queue; no consecutive attempts on the same route.
  4. The last minute before time is reserved for score tallying; the judge's record is final.

4. Safety & Equipment

  1. Wear athletic clothing. You may bring your own shoes and chalk bag.
  2. No climbing without climbing shoes. Tie up long hair. No sharp accessories.
  3. Check mats and safety area before climbing. Do not wear climbing shoes outside the climbing area.
  4. If you feel unwell or injured, stop immediately and inform staff.

5. Discipline

  1. Impersonation, falsifying identity, interfering with judges, or tampering with routes is prohibited.
  2. Respect others. No shouting, pushing, insulting, or any form of discrimination.
  3. Violators may receive a warning, have a single route score cancelled, or be disqualified.
  4. The organizer reserves the right of final interpretation.

✍️ Participant's Pledge

I voluntarily register for this competition and pledge:

  1. I have read and understood all rules and will follow the organizer's and judges' instructions.
  2. I will compete under my true identity, without impersonation or falsification.
  3. I will compete fairly and not use any improper means to gain scores or disrupt others.
  4. I will respect the judges' decisions and raise any disagreement through proper channels.
  5. I will respect other climbers, staff, and spectators, and avoid any insulting, discriminatory, or dangerous behavior.
  6. I confirm I am physically fit to participate in climbing and accept the risks of participation.
  7. I consent to the organizer using my name, photos, and competition footage for promotional purposes.
  8. If I violate the above, I accept cancellation of scores or disqualification and bear the corresponding responsibility.
